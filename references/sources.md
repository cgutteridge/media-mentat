# SF/fantasy source defaults

These are generic starting points, not personal endorsements or taste evidence.
Confirm current URLs, coverage and access conditions during a Research pass.
This registry itself does not certify current shortlist membership.

| Source | Role | Coverage / use | Spoiler risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Hugo Awards](https://www.thehugoawards.org/) | list-refresh | Annual categories across books, short fiction, screen and graphic stories | Low on bare lists |
| [Nebula Awards](https://nebulas.sfwa.org/) | list-refresh | Official annual finalists and winners | Low on bare lists |
| [Arthur C. Clarke Award](https://www.clarkeaward.com/) | list-refresh | Official novel shortlists and announcements; verify publishing domain | Low on bare lists |
| [BSFA](https://www.bsfa.co.uk/) | list-refresh | Official annual award categories | Low on bare lists |
| [Locus](https://locusmag.com/) | list-refresh | Its own Awards and Recommended Reading List; secondary for other awards | Low to medium |
| [Clarkesworld](https://clarkesworldmagazine.com/) | list-refresh | Published fiction/podcast RSS; separate short-fiction lane | Medium in descriptions |
| [Escape Pod](https://escapepod.org/) | list-refresh | Official podcast feed; separate short-fiction/audio lane | Medium in descriptions |
| [Reactor](https://reactormag.com/) | discovery | Reviews, new releases, thematic lists | Medium |
| [ISFDB](https://www.isfdb.org/) | discovery | Bibliographies, editions and creator gaps | Varies |
| [Five Books](https://fivebooks.com/) | discovery | Expert lists and interviews | Varies |
| Publisher and creator bibliographies | discovery | Work identity, series order and craft framing | Medium to high in synopses |
| Guardian / New York Times speculative-fiction coverage | discovery | Criticism and roundups | Varies |
| [LibraryThing](https://www.librarything.com/) / [StoryGraph](https://thestorygraph.com/) | discovery | Peer discovery leads requiring verification | Varies |
| Official regional audiobook, publisher and streaming-provider pages | discovery | Edition, narrator, runtime, region and current availability | Medium; avoid synopsis/reviews |
| [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/) indexes | adjacency | Cross-media cousins from a confirmed taste reason | High |
| File 770 / Nerds of a Feather | discovery | Broader awards discussion and screen leads; not official award authority | Medium to high |
| r/printSF / r/Fantasy and media communities | community | Leads only; verify separately | High |
| [Comic-Con Eisner Awards](https://www.comic-con.org/awards/eisner-awards/) | list-refresh; manual | Comics nominees; no packaged parser yet | Low to medium |
| Comics press and publisher lists | discovery | Graphic-fiction discovery | Varies |
| [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/developers/api) | discovery; optional | Work/edition identification; no packaged adapter yet | Low for metadata |
| [TMDB](https://developer.themoviedb.org/) | discovery; optional | Screen IDs and regional discovery; requires own API access | Medium in overviews |
| [Comic Vine](https://comicvine.gamespot.com/api/) | discovery; optional | Comics IDs; requires own API access | Medium to high |

`list-refresh` supplies authoritative membership evidence. `discovery` supports
research questions; `adjacency` supplies cousins; `community` supplies leads.
Keep these roles distinct. Use published feeds/APIs where appropriate and verify
current terms, attribution, access and rate limits before adding automation.
Never bypass access controls or bulk-copy reviews/story text into caches.
