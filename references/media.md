# Adapting to genres and media

Source coverage is extensible. First understand the user's reason for enjoyment
and the current decision, then find appropriate lists and criticism. Use the
[add-source subskill](../skills/add-source/SKILL.md) when the registry lacks the
needed coverage. A medium with no packaged parser can still be researched and
advised on; record source-linked evidence and clearly identify manual research.

Keep medium, genre, subject and taste reasons distinct. A documentary is a screen
form, not a single taste; a game can appeal through mechanics without a substantial
story. Interest in a subject does not establish enthusiasm for every treatment.
Do not impose a narrative-fiction model on all media.

## Gaming

Resolve the title, edition, platform and relevant release/version. When material
to the request, learn hardware/access needs, input preferences, time available,
solo/co-op/multiplayer context and tolerance for difficulty, repetition or pressure.
Do not ask this entire checklist by default. Distinguish mechanics, agency,
progression and social demands from story, presentation and setting.

Use developer/platform documentation for features and requirements, and appropriate
independent reviews for how play feels. Reviews of an earlier release, another
platform or a different mode may not support today's claim. Live-service state,
monetisation, accessibility, server availability and patches are time-sensitive.
Record review version/date and disagreement. Store pages and user reviews are
different kinds of evidence, not interchangeable endorsements. Partial play and
ownership never establish completion or blanket spoiler permission.

## Documentaries and nonfiction

Resolve the film/series, episode, book or edition. Explore subject interest and
preferred treatment: explanatory, investigative, observational, biographical,
essayistic, technical or otherwise. Match pace, depth, emotional intensity and
argumentative style to recorded tastes rather than assume “educational” means fit.

Use official catalogues/festival or broadcaster lists for discovery and independent
criticism for treatment and craft. Enjoyment and factual reliability are separate
questions. Where the advice makes a substantive factual claim or assesses accuracy,
check suitable primary/expert evidence; a distributor's synopsis is not verification.
Flag disputed framing with attribution and uncertainty. Keep revelations and
emotional turns Unread-safe even when the subject is a real event.

## Any new area

Identify the medium's actual decision criteria, a discovery source and sources
capable of supporting fit/friction claims. Register scope and blind spots. Lists
establish inclusion; criticism can support descriptions; neither proves personal
fit. Gather only what the current request needs, then extend the private record
through explicit user feedback. Broader discovery must preserve distinct clusters.
